🔥 Satellite Showdown
Fire data comes from NASA FIRMS (MODIS and VIIRS active fire products). This is an independent educational project and is not an official NASA product. Detections are not confirmed fires.

How it works

Playing

Load your FIRMS CSV files (or use the demo), then guess which sensor wins each round. Correct guesses earn 10 points. When the sensors turn out at least 80% alike, you earn 5 teamwork points. The final screen shows where they agree.

The two sensors

MODIS sees fires at about 1 km resolution. VIIRS sees about 375 m, so it usually finds more and smaller fires. VIIRS files can come from several satellites (Suomi NPP, NOAA-20 “J1”, NOAA-21 “J2”).

Words you’ll see

  • FRP: fire radiative power in megawatts, a measure of how intense a fire is.
  • Brightness: the heat signal in kelvin (K).
  • Confidence: how sure the sensor is. Low, nominal or high.
  • Matched: a MODIS and a VIIRS hotspot within 2 km of each other.
  • UTC hour: detection time in universal time, not Alaska time.

Getting data

Download the 24-hour country or region CSV files from the NASA FIRMS active fire page. Winter files are often empty because few fires burn then.

Privacy

Your files never leave your device. Only your best score is saved, in this browser.
